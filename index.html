01The insight most teams miss

A nearest-neighbour coupling Z_i Z_{i+1} needs a CNOT between connected qubits. When the chip lacks that edge, the compiler inserts SWAPs — each costing 3 extra CNOTs — deepening the circuit and multiplying noise.

🔬 Processor A — the chord problem

Coupling (1,3) is a chord that skips qubit 2. Routing the chain 0-1-2-3 forces an extra SWAP — routed CX 9 vs 6, depth 33 vs 13.

⬡ Processor B — clean cycle

A heavy-hex lattice containing a clean 6-cycle 0-1-2-3-4-5-6. The identical chain maps with zero SWAPs at native cost.

⚙️ Processor C — simulated proposed

A simulated, proposed 6-qubit open path: 4 qubits run the VQE, 2 are reserved (unused). Matches B's cost with fewer qubits. No mitigation benefit claimed.

03Ablation study — chain-compatible vs edge-deficient

To test whether the result holds beyond a single topology, we compared two coupling graphs under identical settings: same Hamiltonian, ansatz, optimized parameters, noise model, shot budget, and seeds. Only the coupling graph changed.

Chain-compatible (baseline)

(0,1),(1,2),(2,3),(3,4),(4,5)

6 CX · depth 13 · 0 SWAP

Supports all required NN interactions natively

Edge-deficient

(0,1),(2,3),(3,4),(4,5),(0,5)

22 CX · depth 48–49 · 6 SWAP

Removes edge (1,2), adds bridge (0,5); graph stays connected but forces longer routing

Measured result (g sweep, 3 seeds each): The edge-deficient topology required +16 CX (≈3.7×), +35–36 depth, +6 SWAP overhead. In every tested value of g ∈ {0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5}, its noisy VQE energy error was 0.22–0.26 Ha higher than the chain-compatible topology's. This is a measured association for this workload and these two topologies; we report the co-occurrence rather than claiming universal causation. Full data: results/ablation_results.csv.

06Honest methodology

✅ What we measured

Exact diagonalization gives ground truth (-4.7588 Ha, independently verified). Cost is measured by transpiling onto each real coupling map with a fixed seed_transpiler=42. Noisy energy uses identical noise + 8192 shots over 3 seeds, reported with σ.

⚖️ Honest framing

Logical 2Q, routed CX, depth and SWAP are each reported separately — never inferred from one another. Ideal accuracy is identical across chips by construction; the signal is the routed cost and the measured noisy-energy gap. The claim is scoped to this 4-site workload only.
