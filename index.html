01The insight most teams miss

A nearest-neighbour coupling Z_i Z_{i+1} needs a CNOT between connected qubits. When the chip lacks that edge, the compiler inserts SWAPs — each costing 3 extra CNOTs — deepening the circuit and multiplying noise.

🔬 Processor A — the chord problem

Coupling (1,3) is a chord that skips qubit 2. Routing the chain 0-1-2-3 forces an extra SWAP — routed CX 9 vs 6, depth 33 vs 13.

⬡ Processor B — clean cycle

A heavy-hex lattice containing a clean 6-cycle 0-1-2-3-4-5-6. The identical chain maps with zero SWAPs at native cost.

⚙️ Processor C — simulated proposed

A simulated, proposed 6-qubit open path: 4 qubits run the VQE, 2 are reserved (unused). Matches B's cost with fewer qubits. No mitigation benefit claimed.

03Ablation study — chain-compatible vs edge-deficient

To test whether the result holds beyond a single topology, we compared two coupling graphs under identical settings: same Hamiltonian, ansatz, optimized parameters, noise model, shot budget, and seeds. Only the coupling graph changed.

Chain-compatible (baseline)

(0,1),(1,2),(2,3),(3,4),(4,5)

6 CX · depth 13 · 0 SWAP

Supports all required NN interactions natively

Edge-deficient

(0,1),(2,3),(3,4),(4,5),(0,5)

22 CX · depth 48–49 · 6 SWAP

Removes edge (1,2), adds bridge (0,5); graph stays connected but forces longer routing

Measured result (g sweep, 3 seeds each): The edge-deficient topology required +16 CX (≈3.7×), +35–36 depth, +6 SWAP overhead. In every tested value of g ∈ {0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5}, its noisy VQE energy error was 0.33–0.59 Ha higher than the chain-compatible topology's. This is a measured association for this workload and these two topologies; we report the co-occurrence rather than claiming universal causation. Full data: results/ablation_results.csv.

Left: routed CX count (chain 6 vs edge-deficient 22). Middle: noisy energy error vs exact (edge-deficient consistently higher). Right: SWAP count (0 vs 6).

07Validation & reproducibility

✅ Reference & correctness

Ground truth: exact diagonalization of the 4-site TFIM Hamiltonian (E₀ = −4.758770 Ha at g=1.0), computed with numpy.linalg.eigvalsh and independently verified against a term-by-term Pauli construction. Ideal VQE reaches 10⁻³–10⁻⁴ Ha error with fidelity 0.998–1.000.

🎚️ Noise assumptions

Depolarizing noise read from the authoritative processor JSON files: 0.5% per CX, 0.1% per 1Q gate (matching the challenge spec). Same noise model for every processor in every comparison — no processor gets a gentler noise budget.

🎲 Shots & seeds

8192 shots per measurement group, 3 seeds (1, 2, 3) per data point. σ is the sample standard deviation across those three seeds — a preliminary variability estimate, not strong statistical proof. Error bars in plots are ±1σ. Cost metrics use a fixed seed_transpiler=42.

🧩 What is official vs ours

Processor A & B: official challenge benchmarks (coupling maps + noise from the PDF spec). Chain-compatible vs edge-deficient: our custom topology ablation (clearly labelled, not part of the official benchmark). Processor C: our simulated proposed architecture — 4 qubits run the VQE, 2 reserved, no mitigation benefit claimed.
